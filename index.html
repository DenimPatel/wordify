Under the hood

How it works

Three simple ideas make the whole thing possible.

1

Keypad mapping

Every digit 2–9 maps to a set of letters, just like a phone keypad (2 → ABC, 7 → PQRS, and so on).

2

Combination search

For every possible starting position, Wordify expands the digits after it into every letter combination they could spell.

3

Dictionary matching

Each combination is checked against a Trie of common English words — including combinations of multiple words — to find real matches.
